## Headline verdict

**Mostly pass with three material discrepancies and two interpretive gaps**, all triangulated by ≥ 3 code paths.

The refinement is real — R-free dropped from 0.396 to ~0.21 (oracle), geometry transformed from severely strained to publication-quality. But applying the skill's 7-step checklist surfaces:

1. **R-free is reproducibly ~0.21, not 0.199.** Three independent code bases — `phenix.model_vs_data` (0.211), `gemmi sfcalc` (0.217), `REFMAC5` (0.213) — converge on R-free in [0.211, 0.217]. Agent's 0.199 is below all three. The < 0.05 R-free-gap success criterion fails by every oracle.
2. **Round 6 has a better R-free than round 7** (oracle 0.207 vs 0.213). The "round 7 NCS torsion restraints reduced the gap from 0.053 to 0.050" claim is the wrong direction by oracle.
3. **Asn A 39 is a Ramachandran outlier** confirmed by **three** code bases including the non-cctbx ProSMART. Agent's "0.00% Rama outliers" rounded a real outlier to zero.
4. **Density-peak inventory is incomplete.** Oracle finds 23 positive peaks > 4 σ (max 6.18 σ near Glu A 54); agent reported 9 (max 4.98 σ). Five oracle peaks above 5 σ are not in the agent's table.
5. **Ca²⁺ identity is interpretive.** No anomalous data exist in `1sar.mtz`; Mg²⁺ would give very similar geometry. The "this is Ca²⁺" claim is consistent with the data but not uniquely so.

The geometry verdict (clashscore < 5, MolProbity < 2, no Cβ deviations, bond/angle RMSZ both < 1 σ against the CCP4 dictionary) is robust under all eight oracles.
