Three discrepancies still hold:

1. **R-free is reproducibly ~0.21**, not 0.199 (PHENIX 0.211, gemmi 0.217, REFMAC5 0.213). The < 0.05 R-free-gap criterion fails under the catalog oracle of record.

- **Density-peak inventory is incomplete** — oracle finds 23+ / 8− peaks > 4σ; agent listed 9. Five peaks > 5σ not surfaced.

The geometry verdict is robust under all ten oracles. **The data verdict is also clean** under the new T13 oracle: no twinning, no tNCS, mild anisotropy (ΔB 7.3 Å²), one borderline ice ring at 3.44 Å (Z=5.21, completeness 1.00 — informational, not a refinement blocker).
